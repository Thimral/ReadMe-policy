Privacy Policy for ReadMe

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data collection (none)

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your documents, scans, recognized text, or any personal or device information.

2. Local storage only

Everything you scan, import, annotate or sign is stored only on your own device, in the app's private storage and a local SQLite database. We have no servers and no access to your files.

3. On-device processing

Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device using Google ML Kit models. Your images are not uploaded for recognition. Scanning uses Google's document scanner, which runs through Google Play services, and your camera only while you are capturing a document; importing uses the files you pick. The app asks for internet access for one reason only: so Google's ML Kit can download its text-recognition model to your phone the first time it is needed. That download brings a model to your phone; none of your documents, images or text is sent out by the app.

4. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

5. Payments

Buying and restoring ReadMe Pro go through Google Play and the Google Play Billing Library. Google handles your payment details and tells the app whether you have bought Pro. We never see your card or account details, and Google's own privacy policy applies to that part.

6. Sharing, rating, the widget and recent apps

Documents leave your device only when you export or share them, through the share sheet you choose. After that, the receiving app controls them. Rating the app hands over to another app only when you choose: four or five stars open the Google Play page so you can leave a public review, and a lower rating with a comment opens an email draft to the developer. The comment is only sent if you send that email, so please do not put anything from your documents in it. The optional home screen widget shows how many documents and pages you have, worked out on your phone, and has buttons that open the app. "Hide ReadMe in recent apps" keeps the app out of the recent-apps list and screenshots. Android's own backup is switched off for this app, so your documents are not copied to the cloud or to a new phone.

7. Children's privacy

ReadMe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / POPIA)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, rectify, or delete. To remove your documents, delete them in the app, use "Erase everything" in More, or uninstall the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever add an optional cloud feature, we will flag it clearly in the app. This version covers the fully offline app.
